## 圖 1：Innovation reminders

1. Beware of our own “Evolutionary traps”
2. Disruptions are often being opposed especially initially
3. Not just satisfaction but focus on Customer Experience
4. Customer is King; do not listen to everything they tell you
5. Creativity originates from a conflict of ideas
6. Accommodate contradictions, ambiguities, inexactness, naïveness when brainstorming new ideas
7. Culture and trust are always pivotal to innovation success
8. Always aim for sustainable and responsible innovations
9. Increasing pressure to innovate in a rapidly diminishing timeframe

### 中文意思

- 小心企業因為去成功經驗而陷入「演化陷阱」。
- 顛覆性改變在初期通常會被反對。
- 不只追求顧客滿意，更要重視整體顧客體驗。
- 顧客很重要，但不應不加批判地照單全收所有顧客意見。
- 創意往往來自不同、甚至衝突的想法碰撞。
- 腦力激盪時要容納矛盾、模糊、不精確，甚至看似天真的想法。
- 文化與信任是創新成功的關鍵。
- 創新應追求可持續及負責任。
- 創新的時間壓力愈來愈大。

## 圖 2：Key innovation ideas

1. Global trends & driving forces are impacting technology development and adoption.
2. Many people are still operating with a mindset in the 3rd Industrial Revolution.
3.	Moore’s Law — computational resources are multiplying & costs are coming down
4.	In the digital economy, success is very much about “connecting the dots”
5.	Data is the new currency
6.	Data + Algorithms + Integration + Automation + Real time represent awesome power
7.	A good idea often has a “WOW” moment!!!
8.	Individuals are smart but organisations are dumb!!!
9.	Marketing and Innovation are the two most important focus for business success
10.	Sooner or later, a successful firm will face Innovator’s Dilemma & Disruptive Innovation
11.	Innovation has evolved from closed to open and now to data-driven digital intelligence
12.	Culture and trust are always pivotal to innovation success

### 中文意思
•	全球趨勢與驅動力會影響技術的發展與採用。
•	很多人仍以第三次工業革命的思維來面對今天的問題。
•	根據 Moore’s Law，運算資源持續增加，而成本持續下降。
•	在數位經濟中，成功很大程度取決於能否「連結不同的資訊與資源」。
•	數據是新的貨幣。
•	數據、演算法、整合、自動化與即時性結合後，會形成強大的競爭力。
•	好點子通常帶有令人驚喜的「WOW moment」。
•	個人可能很聰明，但組織有時反而反應慢、受制度限制。
•	行銷與創新是企業成功的兩大重點。
•	成功企業遲早會面對「創新者的兩難」與顛覆式創新的威脅。
•	創新已從封閉式創新，演變為開放式創新，再走向數據驅動的數位智慧。
•	文化與信任始終是創新成功的重要基礎。
